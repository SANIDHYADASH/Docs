# Smart Books - Accounting, Inventory & Invoicing Platform

A multi-tenant, full-featured invoicing and accounting SaaS designed for Indian businesses with GST compliance, multi-company support, role-based access, Razorpay payments, and a rich set of business reports.

---

## 1. Billing & Invoicing
- Create GST sales invoices and purchase bills in seconds.
- CGST / SGST / IGST split automatically based on your state and your customer's state.
- Prices with tax or without tax — your choice, per item.
- Extra charges (freight, packing, TCS) and extra discounts (trade, loyalty) — before or after tax.
- Automatic round-off, amount in words, and UPI QR code on the bill.
- Type an amount and the app works backwards to the rate — no calculator needed.
- Bill in any currency (optional) with live exchange rates.
- Work on several bills at the same time in different tabs.
- Voice billing: say "5 kg rice and 2 cement" and the lines are added.
- Barcode billing: scan with your phone camera or a USB scanner.
- AI invoice upload: drop a PDF or photo of a bill and the whole invoice is read for you (see section 3A).


## 2. Print Formats & Design

## 3A. AI Invoice Upload & Reading
- Upload a supplier bill or a sale invoice as a PDF, JPG or PNG (up to 8 MB) — drag and drop or pick a file.
- AI reads the party name, GSTIN, state, phone, email and address.
- It also reads the invoice number, date, every line item (name, HSN, unit, quantity, MRP, rate, discount, GST %), taxes, round-off, grand total and amount already paid.
- The normal sale or purchase billing screen then opens **already filled in** — no separate popup, nothing saved until you press save.
- Party and item names are matched to your existing records where possible, so stock and ledgers stay correct.
- Everything stays editable: check the numbers, fix anything the scan got wrong, then save as a normal invoice.
- The uploaded file is never stored — it is only streamed to the reader and discarded.
- Works from Sales → Import and Purchases → Import.
- Keeps working under load: several AI providers can be configured and are tried one after another if one is rate limited or out of quota.
